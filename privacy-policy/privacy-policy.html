Introduction


Demaze is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our marketing automation.



Information we collect


We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, account details, usage data, device information, and cookies to improve our services and user experience.



How we use your information


We use your information to provide services, improve performance, ensure security, communicate updates, and deliver a better experience.



Cookies & tracking technologies


Demaze uses cookies and similar technologies to analyse traffic, remember preferences, and enhance user experience.



How we share information


We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared with trusted partners only to operate our services, comply with laws, or protect security.



Data security


We use industry-standard security systems, encryption, and monitoring tools to protect your data from unauthorized access.



Your rights


You can access, update, or delete your information anytime by contacting our support team.



Changes to this policy


We may update this privacy policy from time to time. All changes will be posted on this page.

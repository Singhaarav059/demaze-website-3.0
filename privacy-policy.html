Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025. This Privacy Policy describes how Demaze Technologies collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you visit our website or engage our software engineering services.

What We Collect

We may collect personal identification information including but not limited to: your full name, work email address, company name, phone number, and technical project scope details when you book a discovery call or submit an inquiry via our contact form.

How We Use Your Data

We utilize the collected data to provide, maintain, and optimize our AI consulting and engineering services, communicate directly with you regarding project deliverables, generate personalized feasibility assessments, and fulfill contractual obligations.

Sharing & Security

Demaze Technologies will never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. We enforce strict enterprise confidentiality, sign comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before engagement, implement encrypted data transmission, and adhere to GDPR and CCPA data privacy principles.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or request complete deletion of any personal or enterprise data stored with us at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact contact@demazetech.com.

Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies strictly necessary to provide seamless navigation, maintain session preferences, and gather anonymized performance telemetry to optimize our site speed and user experience.

Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational improvements or regulatory compliance updates. Any modifications become effective immediately upon publication on this page.
